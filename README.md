# ⌁ Desko - Turn Any Spare Phone Into a Smart Desk Dashboard

## 🚀 What Is Desko?

Desko is a free app that lets you turn an old phone, tablet, or any device with a web browser into a live dashboard for your computer. It shows **music lyrics**, **PC performance stats** (CPU, RAM, disk), **git status**, **a beautiful clock**, and a **Pomodoro timer**—all updated in real time over your WiFi.

No coding. No complicated setup. Just one click to download and run.

---

- **Remote workers** who want a second screen for quick info
- **Developers** who need git status at a glance
- **Music lovers** who want karaoke-style lyrics while working
- **Anyone** with an old smartphone or tablet sitting unused

## 🛠️ How to Use

### 1. Start Desko on Your PC
Double-click the Desko icon. A small window will appear with a **QR code** and a **URL** (like `http://192.168.1.42:5000`).

### 2. Open on Your Phone
Open the camera app on your old phone and scan the QR code. Or, type the URL into your phone's browser.

### 3. Choose Your Dashboard
Once connected, you'll see a clean interface. Select what you want to display:
- **Music** – plays song lyrics when you're listening on your PC
- **Vitals** – shows live stats
- **Git** – shows your current project status
- **Clock** – full-screen clock
- **Pomodoro** – timer you control from your PC

### 4. Control from Your PC
All features are controlled from the Desko window on your PC. For example, start the Pomodoro timer by clicking "Start" on your PC, and the phone will display the countdown.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Phone can't connect
- Make sure both devices are on the **same WiFi network**
- Temporarily disable any firewall or antivirus that might block local connections
- Restart Desko

**Problem:** No lyrics appear
- Desko needs to detect music playing on your PC. Make sure your music app is open and playing
- Not all music apps are supported—try Spotify or Windows Media Player

**Problem:** Stats don't update
- Check that your PC is not in sleep mode
- Refresh the phone browser page

## 🙋 FAQ

**Q: Is this free?**
A: Yes, completely free and open source.

**Q: Do I need to install anything on my phone?**
A: No. Just use the web browser that's already on the phone.

**Q: Will it work on iOS?**
A: Yes, any device with a web browser works—iPhone, iPad, Android, even another computer.

**Q: Does it work over the internet?**
A: No, it's designed for local WiFi only. For privacy and speed, everything stays on your home network.
